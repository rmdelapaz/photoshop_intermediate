  • Print-ready PDFs throughout: hand out or project

Why Instructors Choose It

  • Minimal prep to teach. The facilitator guide scripts each lesson: timing, demos, and the mistakes intermediate users actually make. Walk in and deliver.
  • The step from competent to professional. It picks up where a beginner course stops, and every session ends in the kind of deliverable a client or employer asks for: a hard cutout, a soft-proofed grade, a campaign in every size, a data-driven batch.
  • Diagnose before you edit. Histograms, channels, color settings and light direction are read before a single slider moves, a habit threaded through all eight sessions.
  • Current and responsible. Generative tools are taught as raw material for compositing, with limits, ethics, licensing and disclosure. Retouching is taught with consent and restraint.
  • No image licensing headaches. Labs use generated practice files and learners' own photos, so there's no stock to license for your class.
  • Flexible delivery. Weekly for eight weeks, twice a week, or a 4-day intensive. A natural next course after an introductory Photoshop class.
