Anatomy of a brief · one page

Six parts

  • Objective: one sentence, what and why
  • Audience: who, where, what makes them stop
  • Deliverables + specs: sizes, formats, where they live
  • Constraints: brand, colors, fonts, budget, deadline
  • Success criteria: a checklist someone else could grade
  • References: 6–12, each annotated

L22

Studio Session: Build It (8.2)

Raise the whole image a level at a time.

Work in passes

Block-in · refine · polish

  • 1 Block-in: every element placed, scaled to the horizon; groups named now
  • 2 Refine: color, retouch, light and shadow, masks, type
  • 3 Polish: atmosphere, one unifying grade, details
  • Groups by role, in render order, color-labeled
  • Live adjustments · Smart Objects · Smart Filters
  • Save capstone_v01, v02 each pass + a screenshot
Getting help today

Help flags on your monitor

  • Green: working, all good
  • Yellow: a question when you are free
  • Red: blocked for 5 minutes. Do not sit on it.
CHECK-IN Your success criteria · which pass? · your next move · file health
0:45 · judging your own work

Fresh eyes

  • Flip: View ▸ Flip Horizontal (view only)
  • Squint: zoom to fit; does the focal point survive?
  • Desaturate: a Black & White layer on top, then off
  • Walk away: ten minutes; a night is better
GATE Grade against the brief, not your mood

Present & Case Study (8.3)

Show the thinking, not just the pixels.

The case-study arc

Challenge → approach → process → result → reflection

  • Hero first, big: earn the scroll
  • Outcome-first: "A warm, editorial key art that reads on mobile"
  • Decisions, not every step: three or four moments of judgment
  • Honest before/after; generative assistance named
Assemble · 18 min

In context, then in words

  • Rectangle over the gray area → Convert to Smart Object → place your hero inside
  • Mockups: l23-mockup-wall · -phone · -sleeve
  • firstname-context.jpg + firstname-before-after.jpg → shared folder
  • Write the five beats + your 30-second spoken version
About 4 minutes each

The protocol

  • Present · 90 s: hero in context + the brief in a line + before/after + your 30-second story
  • Squint test · 15 s: everyone squints; what do you see first?
  • Two reviewers · 90 s: read the brief + one rubric row + one glow + one grow
  • Notes · 15 s: presenter writes the grow down
NORMS The work, not the person · point at the pixels · their brief, not your taste
