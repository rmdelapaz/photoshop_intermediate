# Module 1 Labs: Advanced Selection & Masking (Session 1)

Three lessons, each with a guided build (with the instructor) and a "Now You" solo
variation. Each lesson saves its own working file: `luminosity-working.psd` (L01),
`channel-working.psd` (L02), `cutout-working.psd` (L03). The module deliverable is the
L03 build: flowing hair cut out onto a new background with an edge that survives a
saturated test background.

```

├─ README.md                              this file
├─ practice/                              synthetic files (generated by script, safe to redistribute)
│  ├─ l01-high-contrast-landscape.jpg     L01 guided build: near-blown sky over a very dark valley
│  ├─ l01-still-life-window-light.jpg     L01 solo: window-lit still life (highlight glow, midtones)

│  ├─ l03-curly-hair-portrait.jpg         L03 guided build: curly hair + flyaways on a teal studio wall
```

> **Image licensing.** Never use or hand out anything from the course site's `images/`
> folder. Those photos are Adobe Stock licensed to the author for the website only. Labs
> use only the synthetic files below or the learner's own photos.

> **About AI selection on synthetic files.** Select Subject is trained on real photos, so
> on the illustrated L03 sitter it may wobble. That's fine: the fallback (Object Selection
> tool, a box around head and shoulders, then Quick Selection) works reliably, and the
> pipeline's later steps do the real work. Learners' own portraits give the "real" Select
> Subject experience, including the lesson's point that it gives up on the wisps.

## Lab summaries

**L01 · Rescue the Landscape (22 min in class).** `l01-high-contrast-landscape.jpg` →
Image ▸ Mode ▸ 16 Bits/Channel → Save As `luminosity-working.psd` → read the histogram →
Channels: Ctrl/⌘-click RGB (Lights) → Curves layer, pull down (sky) → if it spills,
undo, reload, intersect with Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + Alt/⌥-click RGB → reload, invert
(Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + I), Curves layer, lift (valley) → 100% check along the horizon and the
pines → optional gentle global Curves → Save.
*Solo (start in class, finish at home):* `l01-still-life-window-light.jpg` or own
portrait/still life: glow by lifting only the brightest highlights; Midtones mask (save
Lights and Darks with Select ▸ Save Selection, Select ▸ All, Ctrl/⌘ + Alt/⌥-click each
saved channel to subtract); compare a luminosity-masked sky darken with a hand-painted
mask. *Going further:* record an Action that builds Lights, Darks and Midtones channels.

**L02 · Extract a Tree (22 min in class).** `l02-bare-tree-sky.jpg` → 16-bit, Save As
`channel-working.psd` → click R, G, B; pick Blue → drag Blue to the New Channel icon →
Image ▸ Adjustments ▸ Levels on **Blue copy** only, until sky white and tree black with
twigs intact → Ctrl/⌘-click Blue copy → invert → click RGB → Layers: add layer mask →
File ▸ Place Embedded `l02-new-sky.jpg`, drag below → Place Embedded
`l02-grunge-texture.jpg` on top → Blending Options: This Layer white slider left,
Alt/⌥-split → Save.
*Solo:* `l02-fireworks-on-black.jpg` over `l02-new-sky.jpg` (This Layer black slider
right, split); an intricate subject on a busy background (channel mask + hand painting /
luminosity mask); a portrait's Blue channel (`l03-curly-hair-portrait.jpg` is fine).

**L03 · The Hard Cutout, Module 1 deliverable (32 min in class).**
`l03-curly-hair-portrait.jpg` → 16-bit, Save As `cutout-working.psd` → duplicate the
layer, hide the original → Select ▸ Subject (+ Quick Selection) → Select and Mask
(Ctrl/⌘ + Alt/⌥ + R): Smart Radius, Radius up a few px, Refine Hair, Refine Edge Brush
along the hairline, Smooth/Feather low, Shift Edge slightly negative → Output To: New
Layer with Layer Mask (Decontaminate off) → channel rescue: best hair channel (Blue or
Green on the teal wall), duplicate, Levels, Ctrl/⌘-click, invert, click the layer mask
thumbnail, paint white soft and low-opacity along the hairline → reopen Select and Mask on
the rescued mask: Decontaminate Colors, raise Amount, Output To: New Layer with Layer
Mask → saturated Solid Color layer behind to test → place `l03-new-background.jpg` → Save.
*Solo (homework):* `l03-hair-busy-background.jpg` or own busy-background hair; a glass,
fern or pet; one cutout on a dark and one on a light background.

**Definition of done (Module 1 deliverable):** fine strands kept, no colored halo against
a saturated test background, a soft (not razor) edge; output as a layer + mask with the
original kept as a hidden backup; placed on a new background; `cutout-working.psd` saved.

Hand out `instructor/l03-hair-cutout-reference.png` only to a learner whose cutout has
collapsed late in L03, so they can still practice the pressure test. It's a catch-up
file, not a shortcut.

## Learners' own photos: what to bring to Session 1

Ask in the pre-course email (a week ahead), and ask for the files **on the laptop**, not
only on a phone:

1. **A high-contrast landscape:** a bright (even partly blown) sky over a darker
   foreground. Sunsets, mountains, city streets at golden hour. L01.
2. **A portrait or still life** with a clear light source, for the highlight glow and
   midtones exercise. L01 solo.
3. **A bare tree, power lines, a fence, or another intricate subject against a plain
   bright sky.** L02.
4. *(Optional)* **Flames, smoke, fireworks, or light trails on a dark background**, plus a
   scene to put them in. L02 solo.
5. **A portrait with loose or curly hair against a reasonably plain background.** L03.
6. *(Optional, homework)* **Hair or a pet against a busy background**, and **a glass** or a
   **potted fern**. L03 solo.

Portraits of other people: get their permission first, prefer family, friends, or
self-portraits, and don't share another person's cutout publicly without their consent.
No suitable photos? Free photos from sites such as **Pexels** or **Unsplash** work after
checking the current license on the photo's page; never pull images from a web search.
Anyone without photos uses the synthetic files, which are built to work.

## Practice file specs (for the generator script)

Generated by `labs/_parts/m1.py` (stitched into `labs/build_practice_files.py`). All
files are sRGB (embedded profile), 8-bit, no EXIF; JPEGs are quality 90. Coordinates in
pixels from the top-left. "Noise" = per-pixel Gaussian noise, clipped to 0–255. Each file
uses a fixed seed derived from its name, so regenerated files are identical. The JPEGs are
deliberately 8-bit: converting to 16-bit is the first step of every build.

### L01 · Luminosity Masks

**`l01-high-contrast-landscape.jpg`** · 2400 × 1600
- **Sky (y 0–820):** very bright vertical gradient RGB (214, 229, 244) → (240, 244, 248)
  → (251, 251, 248) at the horizon, plus a soft sun hotspot at (1830, 250) that clips to
  pure white in a small core. Nine cloud blobs (soft ellipse clusters, blur ~28 px) in
  near-white (253, 253, 251) with slightly grayer undersides (214, 222, 232): the cloud
  detail lives in the top ~15 levels, so a Lights-masked curve pulled down makes it appear.
- **Far range:** hazy sine-wave ridge around y ≈ 760, RGB (176, 186, 200) → (150, 160, 172):
  bright midtones that a broad Lights mask catches and an intersected one spares.
- **Middle range:** ridge around y ≈ 890, RGB (104, 112, 116) → (70, 78, 76) with mottling:
  true midtones.
- **Foreground valley (y ≈ 1060 down):** deep shadows RGB (30, 38, 26) → (13, 17, 12) with
  low-frequency mottling, fine grass noise, a winding path (40, 37, 30) and 18 flat rocks
  (38, 38, 35). All of this sits in the bottom ~30 levels, invisible until the Darks curve
  lifts it.
- **Pale cabin** at x 1500–1690, y 1060–1215: wall (192, 178, 156), dark roof and windows.
  It plays the lesson's "bright shirt below the sky": a broad Lights mask darkens it, an
  intersected one leaves it alone.
- **Six dark pines** (14, 21, 16) on both edges rising well into the sky: a hand-painted
  mask's nightmare and a luminosity mask's showcase at 100%.
- **Noise:** sigma 2.2. Measured luminance percentiles (5/25/50/75/95): about 17 / 24 /
  162 / 246 / 254, i.e. a histogram piled at both ends, exactly what Step 1 diagnoses.

**`l01-still-life-window-light.jpg`** · 2000 × 1400
- Dark wall (44, 38, 34) with light falling off from a window at the upper left; a wooden
  table from y = 1000 with grain.
- **Objects (sphere-shaded, lit from the upper left):** a cream ceramic jug (222, 208, 184)
  at (900, 760) with a bright specular hotspot; a dark green glass bottle with a thin
  specular streak; two lemons (232, 190, 52) and a pear (150, 170, 70); a pale linen
  cloth over the left of the table; soft cast shadows to the right.
- Purpose: a clear highlight region (jug hotspot, speculars, cloth) for the Lights "glow",
  plenty of midtones (jug body, table, fruit) for the Midtones contrast, and deep shadows
  that must stay untouched. Noise sigma 2.

### L02 · Channel Masking & Blend-If

**`l02-bare-tree-sky.jpg`** · 2400 × 1600
- **Sky:** vertical gradient RGB (74, 132, 214) at the top → (168, 200, 236) at y ≈ 1260,
  with six faint high-cloud wisps (236, 242, 250) at 45%.
- **Ground:** a dark hedge line (52, 64, 48) and a brown field (92, 82, 52) → (58, 50, 34)
  from y ≈ 1270 down.
- **Tree:** trunk base at (1172, 1380) with root flare; recursive branching (2, sometimes
  3, children per branch, up to 12 levels, extra twiglets), trunk width 46 px tapering to
  sub-pixel twigs drawn at 3× supersampling. Bark RGB (54, 44, 38) with slight variation.
- **Channel separation (the point of the file):** Blue channel sky ≈ 210–236 vs tree ≈ 38:
  near-perfect. Green is fair (≈ 130–200 vs 44). Red is poor near the top (≈ 74 vs 54),
  so channel-shopping has a clear winner. The hedge and field go black with the tree, so
  the ground is kept and only the sky is replaced.
- Noise sigma 2.5.

**`l02-new-sky.jpg`** · 2400 × 1600
- Sunset gradient RGB (38, 46, 96) → (132, 84, 120) → (228, 124, 86) → (252, 186, 104) at
  y ≈ 1300; twelve dusky cloud bands with warm lit edges. Same size as the tree file, so
  Place Embedded drops it in without scaling. Also the backdrop for the fireworks solo.

**`l02-grunge-texture.jpg`** · 2400 × 1600
- Off-white paper (246, 243, 236) with three scales of low-frequency stains (only the
  darkest parts of each noise field show, slightly warm), ~2,600 dark speckles (mostly
  0.6–2.2 px, a few up to 9 px) and ~110 thin curved scratches, in dark gray-brown.
  Monochrome noise sigma 3.
- Purpose: the lesson's Step 4. With This Layer's white slider pulled left, the white paper
  disappears and only the speckle, scratches and stains print over the scene; splitting the
  slider keeps the pale stain edges soft.

**`l02-fireworks-on-black.jpg`** · 2400 × 1600
- Near-black night (5, 6, 12) with four radial bursts (gold, ice blue, pink, lime), each
  ~170 drooping streaks with a soft glow and bright white tips. Noise sigma 2.
- Purpose: the solo's "Blend-If the other direction". This Layer's black slider drops the
  black sky; splitting it keeps the glow smooth.

### L03 · The Hard Cutout

**`l03-curly-hair-portrait.jpg`** · 1600 × 2000
- **Background:** plain studio teal RGB ≈ (112, 188, 192) with a radial falloff toward the
  edges (darker corners).
- **Sitter (illustrated):** head-and-shoulders, face ellipse centered (800, 1020), sphere-
  shaded skin lit from the upper left, simple eyes, brows, nose, lips, blush; neck; a rust
  knit sweater (176, 84, 58) across the bottom.
- **Hair:** dark brown (58, 36, 24) with a warm sheen (128, 86, 52) on the lit side; a
  curly bob mass down to the shoulders whose silhouette is built from ~620 small spiral
  curls on the outline, curly bangs and side locks over the face, ~2,600 interior curl
  strokes for texture, and a gap of teal between the side locks and the neck.
- **Flyaways:** ~280 fine strands (0.9–1.8 px, 35–85% opacity, 40–260 px long, wavy)
  springing off the outline, tinted 30% toward the teal backdrop in this file. That tint
  is the fringe Decontaminate Colors must remove; the thin, semi-transparent strands are
  what Select Subject and the first refine pass miss and the channel rescue brings back.
- **Channel separation:** Blue ≈ 160–190 (backdrop) vs ≈ 15–35 (hair) and Green
  ≈ 155–185 vs ≈ 25–55 are strong; Red ≈ 90–112 vs ≈ 45–80 is weak. Noise sigma 2.

**`l03-new-background.jpg`** · 1600 × 2000
- Warm evening gradient (70, 52, 60) → (150, 98, 74) → (96, 64, 52) with ~90 soft bokeh
  discs (warm whites, oranges, a few violets) and a light overall blur. Same size as the
  portrait. A warm scene on purpose: any teal fringe left on the hair shows immediately.

**`l03-hair-busy-background.jpg`** · 1600 × 2000
- The same sitter geometry with **auburn** hair (132, 62, 30) and a copper sheen, a blue
  sweater, on a **busy** out-of-focus foliage backdrop: ~170 blurred discs of greens,
  pale yellow-white and tan over a green gradient. No single channel separates the hair
  everywhere (the pale bokeh discs are bright in every channel, the dark greens are dark in
  Red), so the solo has to combine the channel rescue with hand painting.

**`instructor/l03-hair-cutout-reference.png`** · 1600 × 2000, **RGBA** (instructor only)
- The sitter from `l03-curly-hair-portrait.jpg`, rendered from the same geometry on a
  fully transparent background, flyaways in their true (un-tinted) color with correct
  partial alpha: the ideal decontaminated result. Derived by rendering the subject over
  black and over white and solving for alpha, so edge transparency is exact.
- Use: show the target edge, or let a learner who has fallen behind practice the
  saturated-background pressure test. Keep it off learner machines until then.
