Ps From Practitioner to Pro

You can already drive Photoshop. This course teaches you to work like a professional: selections driven by the image itself, real color management, high-end retouching, commercial compositing, and automation that scales. Fewer first steps, much deeper craft.

📚 What This Course Is

This is the sequel to the intro course. It does not re-teach layers, masks, or the basics of Camera Raw and Curves. Instead, every technique you met once at a basic level returns here as a full professional workflow, and three genuinely new pillars appear: color management, luminosity and channel masking, and data-driven, scripted automation.

The teaching model is unchanged: project-driven and spiral. Every module ships a finished, portfolio-worthy deliverable, and the four ideas that run all of Photoshop (non-destructive editing, the layer stack, masks as grayscale, and selections) keep returning at greater depth.

By the end you will be able to…

  • Build luminosity and channel masks and cut out genuinely hard subjects
  • Manage color properly: working spaces, bit depth, and soft proofing for print
  • Grade with Curves, gradient maps, and LUTs, and match color across images
  • Retouch a portrait to a magazine standard, naturally
  • Build a believable multi-image composite with matched light, shadow, and atmosphere
  • Run design systems, data-driven graphics, and scripted batches

✅ Prerequisite

The intro course, or equivalent comfort with layers, masks, selections, Camera Raw, Curves, Smart Objects, and a basic two-image composite. If any of those feel shaky, spend a little time there first; this course moves fast and assumes them.

🧭 Before You Start

Two minutes of setup keeps the whole course honest. This is not a module (there is nothing to deliver), just the professional baseline you will work from:

  • Work in a sane color pipeline. Edit in 16-bit when you can, and know your working space (sRGB for web, wider gamut for print). We go deep on this in Module 2.
  • Calibrate your monitor if you are doing color-critical work. An uncalibrated screen makes every grading decision a guess.
  • Learn to read the histogram. Before you touch a slider, look at where the tones actually sit. "Diagnose before you edit" is a habit you will use in every module.
  • Set up your workspace and a few keyboard shortcuts for the tools you reach for most. Speed is a professional skill.

Full color-management detail lives in Module 2 · Lesson 1; this is just the orientation.
